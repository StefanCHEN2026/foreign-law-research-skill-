# Foreign Law Ascertainment Research Log (Template)

> Per Art. 12 of the Shanghai Financial Court *Guidelines on the Ascertainment of Foreign Law*: where the court researches on its own, it prepares a work record of search times, paths and keywords, texts obtained, and validity verification.
> **How to use**: copy this template at the *start* of every engagement; log every search action as it happens; deliver it with the Ascertainment Opinion. It proves reproducibility and answers "was the search complete and reliable" challenges.
