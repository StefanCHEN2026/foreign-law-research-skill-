## Usage Notes

### Authority priority
1. **🟢 Official sources** — government sites and official international-organization sites; highest authority
2. **🟡 LII / academic** — high quality, possible update lag
3. **🔵 Third-party platforms** — broad coverage, lower authority; cross-verify

### Accessibility (from mainland China)
- Most international-organization sites (UN, EU, WTO…) are reachable
- Google Scholar (scholar.google.com) is unstable
- Russia's pravo.gov.ru may be unstable
- Prefer official government sites and the LII network

### Language
- International organizations offer multilingual versions (including Chinese)
- National databases are mostly in the official national language; some offer English translations
- Asian databases (Japan, South Korea, Singapore…) mostly provide English translations

### Crawling policy
- Most databases tolerate reasonable access; respect robots.txt
- Some provide APIs and open data (tagged "API available")
- Google Scholar (#16) strictly prohibits crawling
- Jus Mundi (#85) has strict terms of use
