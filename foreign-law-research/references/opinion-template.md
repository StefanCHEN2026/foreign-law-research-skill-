# Foreign Law Ascertainment Opinion (Template)

> Structure per Art. 10 of the Shanghai Financial Court *Guidelines on the Ascertainment of Foreign Law*. Use this skeleton whenever the output is a submission-grade ascertainment.
> Golden rule: **every conclusion traceable, every uncertainty disclosed**. `[...]` marks fill-ins; italics are drafting guidance.
> For judicial submission: review and signature by an expert qualified in the target jurisdiction (Art. 6) are required (Art. 11); agency ascertainment additionally needs the agency seal.
